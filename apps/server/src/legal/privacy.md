# Privacy policy

**DRAFT for legal review.** It is not yet in force. Text in [square brackets] must be completed by the operator.

Naija Street Life ("the game") is operated by [Operator name, RC number, address in Nigeria] ("we"). This policy explains what we collect, why we collect it, and your rights under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA) and the GAID 2025.

## What we collect

- **Game account:** a random guest token, which we store only as a hash. We also store the display name and look you choose, and when you last played.
- **Cloud save:** your game progress, if you connect to a server.
- **Phone verification (optional):** we never store your phone number. We keep only a keyed hash, which lets us recognise the same number later, plus its last two digits for display. The one-time code is sent through our SMS provider [name].
- **Birth year (optional):** used only to check that you are 18 or older for prize features.
- **Things you post:** chat messages, direct messages, gig posts, profile fields, ratings, poll and election votes, and messages to NPCs. Moderators can see these when a report or a safety check needs it.
- **Safety records:** reports, strikes, mutes and bans.
- **Anonymous statistics and crash reports (only if you agree):** a random install ID that is not linked to your account. They record which missions and mini-games were played, frame-rate buckets, and error messages. They include no location, contacts, advertising IDs or device identifiers.

## What we do not collect

We do not collect precise location, contacts, photos, microphone recordings or advertising IDs. We do not sell personal data.

## Why we use it (legal basis)

- **To run the game and your account:** this is our contract with you.
- **To keep players safe:** moderation, fraud and cheat prevention, and enforcing the rules. This is our legitimate interest and a legal obligation.
- **Phone verification and the 18+ check:** with your consent, and to meet legal duties for prize features.
- **Anonymous statistics:** only with your consent, which you can withdraw in Settings at any time.

## AI and news

Some NPC lines are written by an AI service (Google Gemini, on a paid plan that does not use data for training). That service sees news headlines and your messages to NPCs when you use "Talk to NPC". It never sees your phone number or account details. News lines always show their source.

## Sharing

We share data only with providers that run the game for us, under contracts: hosting [provider], SMS [provider], AI (Google), and later prize or payment partners. We may also share it when the law requires.

## How long we keep it

- **Account and save:** until you delete them.
- **Chat:** 12 months.
- **Moderation records:** 24 months.
- **News items:** 7 days after they expire.
- **Statistics:** 13 months.
- **Election vote receipts:** kept, but anonymised when you delete your account.

## Your rights

You can access, export, correct or delete your data, object to processing, and withdraw consent.

- **In the game:** use Profile → "Download my data" and Profile → "Delete my account".
- **Otherwise:** contact [privacy contact email].

You can also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

## Age

Online features are for players aged 18 and over. Younger players can play the story offline.
