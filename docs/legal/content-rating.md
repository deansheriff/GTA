# Content rating questionnaire (IARC) answers (draft) ⚖️

**Category:** Game.

| Question area | Answer | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Violence | Yes: mild, cartoon, slapstick | Thrown slippers and jerrycans, chases, bumping cars. No blood, no deaths, no weapons used against people (Story Bible rule). |
| Fear / horror | No | |
| Sexuality / nudity | No | |
| Language | Mild | Pidgin banter such as "wahala" and "mumu" (filtered in player chat). No strong profanity in scripted text. |
| Controlled substances | No | No alcohol or drug use shown. Malt drinks only. |
| Gambling | **No** simulated gambling and **no** real-money gambling | Skill challenges are free entry with no stake. Prize features are off by default and need legal review before they are enabled. |
| Crude humour | Mild | Comedy situations. |
| Discrimination | No | Hate speech is filtered and banned. |
| User interaction | **Yes** | Users can interact and chat, share content (profiles, gigs), and see others' content. Moderated: pre-send filters, report, block, strikes. |
| Shares location | No | |
| Digital purchases | No | There are no in-app purchases at launch. |
| Unrestricted internet | No | Only the game's server; news links open the outlet in the browser. |

**Target audience (Play "Target audience and content"):** 18+ for online features. The story is suitable for teens, but the app is listed for **18 and over** because of chat, gigs and the prize framework.

**News apps / government apps declarations:** not a news app; news is ambient flavour with sources. Not a government app. The elections are fictional, in-game ward offices.
