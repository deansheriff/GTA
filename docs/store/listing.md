# Google Play listing kit (draft)

**App name (30):** Naija Street Life: Up NEPA!

**Short description (80):** Drive the danfo, hustle Lagos, survive NEPA — a hand-painted Naija comedy adventure.

**Full description:**

Lagos never sleeps. NEPA does.

Naija Street Life is a hand-painted open-world comedy set in a tropical-island Lagos: Yaba,
Oshodi, Surulere, Obalende, Ikoyi, Victoria Island, Lekki and more. Drive "Baba God Dey", the
loudest yellow danfo on the Third Mainland Bridge. Hustle for money, eat amala, sleep, and
pray the light stays on.

**Story mode: "Up NEPA!"**
- A Lekki owambe, a blackout, and a missing bag full of the Chief's money.
- Play three heroes and switch between them in the "Family Meeting 🔥" group chat:
  - **Tunde**, the danfo driver with high blood pressure
  - **Aunty Boss**, the canteen queen whose Stare can stop a debtor at fifty metres
  - **KC Vibes**, the content creator whose battery is always at 4%
- 22 missions, jollof diplomacy, generator wars, a visa interview with an officer who never
  blinks, a ward election, and four endings.

**Live the city**
- Danfo runs, deliveries and POS shifts (the network will fail).
- Needs, money, skills and a phone full of apps.
- Day and night, with NEPA cuts that shape the streets.
- Named characters with their own daily routines.

**Online (18+)**
- See other players on your street and chat. Messages are checked before anyone sees them.
- A gig board in game money; you complete gigs by playing them.
- Ward elections with real effects in the game, and community polls.
- Daily skill challenges and leaderboards.

Every character, business, agency and party is fictional. Real places are settings only.
