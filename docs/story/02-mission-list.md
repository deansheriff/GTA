# Up NEPA! — Mission List (Story Deliverable 2)

*Draft v0.1, 2026-10-14 (M0). Builds on the [Story Bible](story-bible.md).*
*All Pidgin, Yoruba, Igbo and Hausa lines are pending native-speaker review (Bible §10).*

**Labels:**
- Hero: **T** = Tunde, **A** = Aunty Boss, **K** = KC, **All** = switchable co-op
- **₦s** = story money (separate from free-roam money until the end, Bible §9.7)
- **XP** = skill XP; **Rel** = relationship change between heroes

**Length target:** 30 story missions, about 8–10 hours. Each mission is 8–15 minutes
with a mid-mission checkpoint.

---

## Prologue: "Owambe, Owambe" (tutorial; ~20 min; all three heroes)

### P1. "Arrive in Style" — T
**Objective:** drive four wedding guests from Ojota to a Lekki event centre before the couple's entrance.

**Set piece:**
- The guests argue about whose aso-ebi is "original".
- A road warden checkpoint teaches the Conductor Combo: Sule shouts "Owambe guests! VIP!" and the warden waves you through.

**Comedic beat:** one guest insists on stopping to buy *chin-chin* "for the road". It's a 6-minute drive.

**Teaches:** driving, the BP meter, the Conductor Combo (one call).

**Reward:** ₦s 3,000 · Driving XP

### P2. "Jollof Diplomacy" — A
**Objective:** serve 30 plates during the jollof rush without running out, and stop two guests from double-dipping.

**Set piece:** the jollof queue as a timing and priority mini-game. Elders first, the bride's family second, "people who came with their own takeaway pack": never.

**Comedic beat:** a man claims he is "the groom's cousin from abroad". The Stare reveals he's from Ikorodu.

**Teaches:** serving, The Stare, debt reminders (a guest owes her ₦8,000 from the last owambe).

**Reward:** ₦s 5,000 · Cooking XP · Aura +5

### P3. "Content Is Content" — K
**Objective:** film three "viral moments" for KC's channel: money spraying, the MC, and the gele competition.

**Set piece:** the money-spraying rhythm game. Time the spray to the beat; the dancers react.

**Comedic beat:** KC films the bride's mother at the exact second she trips. He deletes it, nervously. (He didn't delete it.)

**Teaches:** camera, framing, Hype, Battery.

**Reward:** Hype +10 · Filming XP

### P4. "NEPA Take Light" — All (forced switching)
**Objective:** in the two-second blackout, each hero does one thing. Then the light returns and the bag is gone.

**Set piece:** the blackout sequence (Bible §6). The camera flips between the three heroes in the dark using **Family Meeting 🔥** chat bubbles. This is where the switching mechanic is taught.

**Comedic beat:**
- Generator "also in the dark".
- "UP NEPAAA!" as everyone freezes mid-pose.

**Ending:** the title card *Naija Street Life: Up NEPA!*

**Reward:** Switching unlocked.

## Act 1: "Light Out" (~2 hours)

### 1.1 "Baba God Dey" — T
**Objectives:**
1. Run 3 passenger loops: Oshodi → Obalende → Oshodi.
2. Collect fares.
3. Survive one go-slow.

**Set piece:** the go-slow on a bridge. **Go-Slow Whisperer** first appears: a hidden market bypass lights up.

**Comedic beats:**
- **The passenger debates** (data-driven): football, fuel price, whose pastor is more powerful.
- A man pays "next time" and leaves a **Ghana-must-go bag** under seat 4.

**Ending:** at the depot, Tunde opens the bag. The music cuts.

**Reward:**
- ₦s 8,000
- Driving XP
- **Ability: Go-Slow Whisperer (tier 1)**
- Item: *The Bag*

### 1.2 "Ajo Day" — A
**Objectives:**
1. Collect ajo contributions from 6 market women.
2. Run the POS through three network failures.
3. Settle the "₦200 dispute".

**Set piece:**
- **POS network failure** mini-game: switch networks, hold the phone at the window, wait for "Transaction pending" to turn into "Approved", and keep the queue calm.
- Fail and the queue riots (comedic).

**Comedic beat:** a debtor tries to pay with "transfer, I've sent it" and shows a screenshot from 2023.

**Ending:** a market woman whispers that the Chief's men are looking for "a canteen woman in the video".

**Reward:**
- ₦s 12,000
- Haggling XP
- **Ability: Ajo Network (tier 1)** (one trusted-keke fast travel per day)

### 1.3 "Four Percent" — K
**Objectives:**
1. KC's blackout clip is at 40,000 viewers.
2. His phone is at **4%**.
3. Find power before it dies, and keep the stream alive.

**Set piece:** a Yaba scavenger run for power:
- the barbershop generator (costs ₦200 plus a haircut opinion)
- a church's inverter (must sit through the end of the prayer)
- a "charging station" kiosk with 30 phones on one socket

**Comedic beat:** KC's signal drops exactly as he reveals the Chief's face. Chat: "??? continue!!!"

**Ending:** a henchman, **Pepper**, recognises KC from the clip. A chase on foot ends with KC hiding in Aunty's canteen.

**Reward:** Hype +20 · **Ability: Go Live (tier 1)** · Battery upgrade: power bank ×1

### 1.4 "Who Get This Bag?" — T
**Objective:** get the bag to somewhere safe without the Chief's people seeing the danfo.

**Set piece:** the first escape chase from Pepper and Salt through Mushin. Passengers **vote** on every turn (preview of 2.3).

**Comedic beat:** a passenger refuses to get down because she has "paid for the full journey". She stays for the entire chase, filing her nails.

**Reward:** ₦s 4,000 · BP control +1

### 1.5 "Family Meeting" — All (convergence)
**Objective:** all three end up in Aunty Boss's canteen at night, with the bag on the table.

**Set piece:** a **dialogue standoff** over generator noise. Raise your voice to be heard, and lose lines if you don't.

**Choice 1: what to do with the bag.**
- **Hide it.** Unlocks the Act 2 "Spread" route as written.
- **Return it.** The Chief's men refuse ("Who send you?"), so the bag stays with the heroes. This sets flag `tried_honest` for Ending A.
- **"Count it first."** A comedy counting mini-game. You discover a **diary page** stuck in the cash, which starts the Ending D trail.

**Reveals:**
- Aunty recognises **her own stitching** on the handle.
- Tunde stays quiet; his secret isn't revealed yet.

**Reward:**
- Rel +10 (all)
- The **"Family Meeting 🔥" chat** becomes the permanent switch menu
- Free-roam opens: Yaba, Lekki, Oshodi

## Act 2: "Spread" (~4 hours; solo arcs + co-op, open order within each block)

### Block A (any order)

**2.1 "Generator Wars" — All**
- **Objective:** move a 60 kVA diesel generator and two jerrycans across three streets at night, past rival streets defending their own.
- **Set piece:** stealth plus slapstick. Rival streets throw sachets and slippers.
- **Comedic beat:** when the generator starts, every street within 1 km gets light. The rivals stop fighting and start dancing.
- **Reward:**
  - ₦s 15,000
  - **Ability: The Stare (tier 2)**: works on groups
  - The canteen gets a permanent generator, so it becomes a **charging hub** for KC

**2.2 "Japa Interview" — K**
- **Objective:** pass the Republic of Northvale visa interview mini-game: quick answers, a composure meter, and documents.
- **Set piece:**
  - The officer never blinks.
  - His questions get absurd: "Describe your relationship with your grandmother's goat."
- **Branch:**
  - **Pass** → flag `japa_possible`.
  - **Fail** → comedic rejection letter; KC can retry in Act 3.
- **Secret beat:**
  - Aunty finds the receipt **if** the player hasn't confessed.
  - **Confess first** → Rel ↑.
  - **Caught** → Aura ↓ and Rel −20. Her silence is the scariest moment in the game.
- **Reward:** Hype +15 · Composure XP

**2.3 "Danfo Chase" — T**
- **Objective:** escape Pepper and Salt through go-slow with 14 passengers who vote on every turn.
- **Set piece:**
  - The **passenger voting** UI. Each passenger has an opinion and a vote weight.
  - The prophetess's "turn of favour" leads into a dead end.
- **Comedic beat:** the chase passes through a wedding convoy; Tunde joins it to blend in.
- **Reward:** ₦s 10,000 · **Conductor Combo (tier 2)** with 3 chained calls

### Block B (unlocks after any two in Block A)

**2.4 "Aso-Ebi Infiltration" — All**
- **Objective:** get into Mama Chief's 90th birthday party in the correct aso-ebi and find Barrister Ledger's office.
- **Set piece:**
  - **Dress-code check** at the gate.
  - **Aunties' interrogation:** dialogue dodging ("When are you marrying?", "How is your mother's leg?").
  - Mama Chief takes a liking to Aunty Boss.
- **Comedic beat:** KC is forced to dance at the centre of the hall while the others search. His Hype goes up; his dignity does not.
- **Reward:** ₦s 20,000 · diary pages ×2 · Rel +5

**2.5 "Following the Money" — A** *(reworked "Small Small Money", Bible §8)*
- **Objective:** use the Ajo Network to trace how Barrister Ledger split the Chief's cash across 40 POS agents, and collect **evidence**: receipts, CCTV stills, testimony.
- **Set piece:** an **investigation board** mini-game. Connect agents, dates and amounts; market women supply clues.
- **Comedic beat:** one POS agent kept a receipt for every transaction "because my mother taught me". She becomes a recurring ally.
- **Teaches no crime:** the player only *uncovers* the structuring and never performs it.
- **Reward:** evidence ×3 · **Ajo Network (tier 2)**: hideouts · Aura +10

**2.6 "Fuel Queue Standoff" — T + A**
- **Objective:** get diesel for the canteen generator at the only open PetroNaija station.
- **Set piece:**
  - A queue-jumping negotiation; The Stare on the pump attendant.
  - A slapstick brawl when a convoy tries to skip the queue. Jerrycans fly; nobody bleeds.
- **Reward:** ₦s 6,000 · BP control +1 · diesel ×2

### Block C (unlocks after 2.4 and 2.5)

**2.7 "Viral" — K**
- **Objective:** use **Gist** and **Go Live** to turn public opinion about the Chief (fictional, in-world).
- **Set piece:** a **rumour graph**. Seed gists through danfo passengers, market women and WhatsApp groups, and watch them spread.
  - **True gists** (backed by evidence) stick.
  - **False gists** can be debunked: Hype crashes, and the ward trusts KC less.
- **Ambient news layer:** NPC banter may reference *real* news topic cards. This is **flavour only** and never affects the mission (Bible §9.5).
- **Reward:** Hype +30 · **Gist (tier 2)**

**2.8 "Midpoint: Old Money" — All**
- **Objective:** a full count of the bag at the canteen.
- **Twist:**
  - The cash is mostly **old, withdrawn notes**; the Chief has been hoarding worthless money.
  - The **real prize** is Barrister Ledger's **diary**.
- **Comedic beat:** Tunde does the maths on his "fleet money" and quietly lies on the floor.
- **Reveal:** Tunde confesses his secret (he drove the Chief's last campaign bus). The others react depending on Rel.
- **Reward:**
  - Diary pages ×3
  - Act 3 unlocked
  - **Choice: keep or hand over the old notes.** This sets up the Ending B path.

## Act 3: "Election Day" (~2 hours)

### 3.1 "Campaign Season" — All (open)
- **Objective:** in the week before the vote, run up to 6 side activities that move **NPC voting blocs**: traders, drivers, students, church women, okada union, youth.
- **Set piece:** the election **single-player simulation** (proposal F4), using the same UI as community elections.
- **Rules:**
  - Bribes are available but **always backfire** (Bible §8.2).
  - Evidence and Teacher's town halls move blocs honestly.
- **Reward:** bloc shifts

### 3.2 "The Debate" — K + A
- **Objective:** get Teacher (the rival candidate) into the televised debate, and make sure the Chief can't skip it.
- **Set piece:** a dialogue battle. Aunty's receipts against the Chief's slogans; KC controls the live chat.
- **Reward:** blocs +10 · Hype +20

### 3.3 "Light Man" — T
- **Objective:** find Engineer Bayo ("Light Man") and convince him to restore power to the rally ground at the exact moment the Chief takes the stage.
- **Set piece:**
  - Fetch the specific suya he likes from across the city before the transformer shift change.
  - A danfo time-trial.
- **Comedic beat:** he refuses money, so you pay in suya ("I'm a professional").
- **Reward:** finale access

### 3.4 "Up NEPA!" — All (finale)
**Objective:** the rally at the ward stadium.
1. Blackout as the Chief begins.
2. The heroes position themselves: Tunde blocks the convoy exit, Aunty takes the mic, KC goes live.
3. On cue, the light returns. Every light, speaker and phone comes on.
4. KC streams the **diary**, and Aunty reads the receipts aloud to the kobo.

**Set piece:** a timing-based finale that switches between all three heroes every 20–30 seconds.

**Ending resolution** (choices across acts):

| Ending | Requires (summary) |
|---|---|
| **A — Honest** | `tried_honest` OR evidence ≥ 5; old notes handed over; family Rel ≥ 60 |
| **B — Hustle** | old notes kept and laundered through "a guy" (off-screen and comedic; fails) |
| **C — Japa** | `japa_possible` and KC chooses to go at the airport scene |
| **D — Secret** | all 10 diary pages; the bag opened with Aunty's stitch-key |

### 3.5 Epilogues
- **A:** transformer and borehole ribbon-cutting; Baba God Dey becomes the ward shuttle; Aunty forgives Tunde's debt in public.
- **B:** an FCIB knock; three people eating separately.
- **C:** a video call from Northvale; "Aunty Boss Kitchen TV" goes viral abroad.
- **D:** the diary names someone *above* the Chief. Sequel tease.
