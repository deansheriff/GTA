# Device QA checklist (D-031)

Play this on real phones before the store build. Note the phone, the Android version and what
happened, and send it back.

## Phones to cover
- A low-end phone: 2–3 GB RAM, Android 9–11. Use the Low preset; it should hold 30 fps.
- A mid-range phone: 4–6 GB RAM, Android 12–13, on Medium.
- A recent phone: Android 14–15, ideally with a notch or a punch-hole camera, on High.

## Boot and loading
- [ ] The loading screen appears within a second or two of the splash. The bar moves and the tips
      change, with no frozen black screen.
- [ ] The menu appears. The title camera drifts smoothly.
- [ ] Travel to a region (missions 11, 16, 17, 23): the loading screen shows, then the region. Coming
      back to Lagos works the same way.

## App switching and interruptions
- [ ] Press Home during free roam, wait 30 s, then come back: the pause menu is showing, and
      Resume works.
- [ ] Do the same during a mission's dialogue and during a fight.
- [ ] Lock the screen with the power button, then unlock it: the pause menu shows.
- [ ] Take a phone call mid-drive: it pauses.
- [ ] The Android back button opens pause, and pressing it again closes pause.
- [ ] Kill the app from Recents mid-mission, then reopen it and press Continue: the mission resumes
      at its last checkpoint.

## Screen and controls
- [ ] Notch or punch-hole: no HUD text or button sits under the camera cut-out. Check portrait-flip
      (landscape left and right).
- [ ] The joystick never sticks: slide your thumb off it, lift your finger outside it, and use
      two fingers at once.
- [ ] Camera drag on the right half: left, right, up and down.
- [ ] Settings → Left-handed controls swaps the sides. Text size 130% still fits.
- [ ] Settings → Battery saver caps at 30 fps; the phone stays cooler after 15 minutes.

## Heat, battery and memory
- [ ] Play 20 minutes of free roam: note the fps on the top line at the start and at the end, and
      whether the phone gets hot.
- [ ] Play 20 minutes of missions with fights and driving: the app doesn't close by itself.
- [ ] Note battery % used per 20 minutes.

## Story spot checks
- [ ] Mission 7: the cell, the yard shootout, and the escape through Yaba.
- [ ] Mission 9: the owambe blend meter, spray, DANCE prompt and photo.
- [ ] Mission 10: the canoe and the Makoko walkways.
- [ ] Mission 12: the okada, boarding the tanker and the roof fight.
- [ ] Mission 16: the horse chase.
- [ ] Mission 17: the speedboat battle and the explosion.
- [ ] Mission 24: stealth in the dark.
- [ ] Mission 26: the yacht boss and the ending.

## What to send back
For any problem, send the phone model, the Android version, the mission or place, what you did,
and a screenshot or screen recording if you can.
